Provenance & Rights

Where the record of this project is held, who holds it, and under what terms. Maintained alongside the public archive at geographiesoftrash.

Institutional Record
The FIU Archive

Geographies of Trash is preserved in the FIU Institutional Repository by the Wolfsonian Public Humanities Lab-FIU, in collaboration with the FIU Ratcliffe Art + Design Incubator, where Ahol held an artist/entrepreneur residency. Eighteen records across four collections, listed here as catalogued. Each links back to its record at FIU; rights statements are those published by the holding institution.
